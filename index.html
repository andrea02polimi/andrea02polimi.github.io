01 / RECOMMENDER SYSTEMS1st Place · ACM RecSys 2026

Conversational music recommendation

Analyzed interaction patterns in a dataset of 388,776 conversational turns and created session-aware validation splits for a setting where 73% of Blind-B target tracks were unseen during training. Compared collaborative filtering, two-tower, and graph-based recommenders to select candidates for the team pipeline. Contributed to the final system, which ranked first on Blind-B with a 0.6889 overall score and 0.6185 nDCG@20, and co-authored the ACM paper.

Learning rewards from human feedback

For my Master's thesis, I built a Python framework combining demonstrations and preferences to train reward models for simulated driving in SUMO. It compares hybrid learning with single-feedback baselines and uses gradient reliability to balance signals.
